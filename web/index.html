⚡ Step-by-Step: How to Use on Flipper Zero:
  1. Fill in your details and click "Download Flipper .nfc File".
  2. Connect your Flipper to your PC (via qFlipper) or phone (via Flipper Mobile App).
  3. Transfer the downloaded file to: SD Card > nfc/
  4. On Flipper: Select NFC > Saved > [Your File] > Emulate.
  5. The Tap Sweet Spot: Touch the top edge of the iPhone against the back of the Flipper. The contact sheet will open immediately!
